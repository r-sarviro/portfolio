Project 01

BabyHelper

Senior React Native Developer · mobile ecosystem for young parents

Context

The product was stuck in a stagnation phase: heavy legacy, outdated SDKs, and a slow delivery cycle.

Challenge

Restore engineering workflows, clear architectural debt, and bring back a predictable performance profile.

What I did

Built estimation, decomposition, and code-review practices; audited and refactored the codebase (removing 15+ incompatible libraries); restructured API requests with backend; planned and shipped New Architecture migration; fixed critical tracking bugs; delivered Dark Mode.

Result

Smaller bundle and ~15× faster cold start; Bridge overhead eliminated; higher App Store and Google Play ratings through stabilized core flows.
